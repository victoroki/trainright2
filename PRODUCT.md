## Product Purpose

TrainRight Technologies is a Kenyan educational technology company. The public website
(trainright.co.ke) is the marketing and onboarding front door for a larger ecosystem:
the TrainRight Digital App (learners), a PC learner portal (app.trainright.co.ke),
a staff portal (staff.trainright.co.ke), an MIS (mis.trainright.co.ke), and an ICT
help desk (ict-support.trainright.co.ke). The website's job is to present services,
drive app downloads and account creation, and host support/policy content.

## Users

- Learners across all Kenyan levels: PP1 to Grade 12, Form 4, TVET, and Teacher
  Education (DTE-PP&P, DSTE), plus parents and guardians.
- Teachers and college trainers (full-time and commission-based content creators).
- Institutions and partners (schools, advertisers, book and laptop sellers).
- Job seekers and vendors (Work with Us).

## Strategic Principles

- Landing page is "Pods of Wisdom": free short videos (10s to 5min max) plus
  priority adverts, and it must push visitors to download the TrainRight Digital App
  (store badges + QR code).
- Every service listed must be a link to the service or a description plus a guide
  on how to access it.
- Separate logins for trainees (learners) and trainers (teachers).
- Payments messaging: MPESA pay bill and bank card; buyers also pay delivery on
  marketplace orders.
- Compliance messaging: Data Protection commitment per the Kenyan Data Protection
  Commissioner (Data Protection Act, 2019).
- Footer must read: (c) TrainRight Technologies 2026, All rights Reserved.
